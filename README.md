## 🎮 About the Game

The Adventurers is a turn-based tactical RPG developed by Eternal Vigilance Entertainment. It launched in Steam Early Access on November 11, 2024, and reached full release on August 31, 2026. The game combines party-based role-playing, strategy, simulation, and open-world exploration in a high-fantasy setting inspired by classic tabletop RPGs.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Adventurers**. This trainer provides quality-of-life and combat assistance for managing an adventuring party, exploring the changing world, completing quests, and surviving turn-based encounters against more than 130 enemy types.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed experience, faster party progression, easier tactical battles, or additional control while exploring quests, events, enemies, classes, and equipment.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after entering the world or a tactical encounter. Party invulnerability and health restoration are useful when positioning adventurers around dangerous terrain or preparing for a difficult engagement. Disable combat-focused options when movement and exploration are complete.

### Tools & Inventory

The game includes class-based character development, customization, and inventory management. Collect equipment and other items during quests and exploration, then organize the party before difficult encounters. Avoid changing inventory-related values while menus are loading or while transferring items.

### World & Environment

The Adventurers features an open, changing world with random events, costly quests, mythical creatures, and points of interest. Use progression assistance to develop the party more quickly, but keep world and quest systems running normally to preserve event flow and save stability.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and tactical. Each adventurer contributes unique abilities, spells, and skills, while terrain and party composition influence the outcome. Activate defensive options before a difficult battle, then use resource and damage options only after combat has fully loaded. Turn all options off before finishing the encounter or saving.
